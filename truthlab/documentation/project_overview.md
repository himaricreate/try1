# TruthLab Project Overview

TruthLab is an AI-guided misinformation simulator that helps users practice identifying suspicious online content. Instead of acting as a perfect detector, it teaches users how to reason through digital content using evidence, source checking, skepticism, and emotional awareness.

## Problem

Online users are exposed to misinformation, phishing attempts, fake screenshots, misleading statistics, and AI-generated content. Many people share content before verifying it.

## Solution

TruthLab simulates a social media/news feed. Users classify posts as Trust, Doubt, or Report. After every decision, the app provides feedback explaining why the answer was correct or incorrect.

## Impact

The platform helps users build digital literacy skills and safer online decision-making habits.
